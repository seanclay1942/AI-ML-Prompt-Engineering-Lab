This lab is intended to to help us create and refine prompts on specific topics, then compare them to another language model. Using a SWOT analysis we will find a company then break it down to better understand it, this both gives us insight into our ideal jobs and creating a well thought out prompt for a well describe answer.

For my company I found "Charles Schwab" and the position "SQL Database Developer - Workplace Services Engineering" from indeed, which database development and managing is my main interest in college, and I have some experience with SQL. Charles Schwab is a massive financial company which provides banking, investing, retirement accounts, and much more. A very big player in the financial world and as time moves forward more and more dependent on Internet and AI tools, creating many opportunities for people with similar interest as me career wise. Being reasonably close to fort worth where I live currently, the experience and pay amount would easily make this an amazing future career.
